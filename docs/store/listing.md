## Tagline

Cut your Figma designs on a Silhouette cutter over Bluetooth.

## Description

Send shapes, text and vectors straight from Figma to your Silhouette cutter. No exporting, no Silhouette Studio.

**What it does**
- Previews your selection on the mat at real size; select a frame to cut everything on it.
- Cuts filled shapes along their outline (holes included), open paths along the path, and text as outlines.
- Click any layer in the preview to skip it for this cut.
- Material presets, plus force, speed, blade depth (AutoBlade) and passes.
- Live progress, and a Stop button that safely resets the cutter.
- Detects your cutter model and uses its cutting width and mat size.

**What you need**
- A Silhouette cutter with Bluetooth. Verified on the CAMEO 4; other models (CAMEO 4 Plus/Pro, CAMEO 5 family,
  Portrait 3/4, Curio 2) are detected but not yet verified.
- The free, open-source Kerf companion, which talks to the cutter over Bluetooth. Figma plugins can't use Bluetooth
  themselves.
  - macOS: the **Kerf for Silhouette** app (signed and notarized).
  - Or the `kerf` command (Node.js), for macOS, Linux or Windows.
- Quit Silhouette Studio while cutting from Figma: the cutter accepts one connection at a time.

Setup guide: https://github.com/keoshi/kerf#setup

Without the companion, you can still preview your design and the exact cutter commands.

**Privacy**
Everything stays on your computer: no accounts, analytics or servers. https://github.com/keoshi/kerf/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
